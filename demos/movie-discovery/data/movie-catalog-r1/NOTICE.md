# Movie Catalog notice

This revision is fully synthetic. Every film title, synopsis, genre, year,
region, runtime, and poster palette in `movies.json` is authored by the
milvus3-demos project for the Movie Discovery (faceted semantic search)
demonstration. The films are fictional and are not derived from any real film,
studio catalog, or public dataset. None of them depict real actors, real
franchises, or existing film companies.

Posters are AI-generated original illustrations produced by the milvus3-demos
project. They are synthetic, fictional artwork (no real actors, likenesses, or
third-party imagery) generated from the authored synopsis and palette of each
movie. They are not photographs of real films and do not depict any real
franchise. The `poster_path` values point to `posters/<movie_id>.jpg`; posters
are generated progressively, so a movie without a generated poster falls back
to a palette placeholder in the UI. When the full set is generated, this notice
and the accompanying manifest will be finalized together.
